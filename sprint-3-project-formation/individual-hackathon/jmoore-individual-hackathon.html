The 6-Point Mandated Policy Pathway

How the Assembly’s consensus blueprint is legally guaranteed to translate into municipal action under the Pre-Commitment Ordinance:

Step 1

Unredacted Public Delivery (Day 0)

The assembly publishes its final consensus blueprint directly to the public and City Council simultaneously with zero administrative edits.

Step 2

Mandatory Legislative Clock (Days 1–60)

Council must hold a formal public work session within 30 days and take a recorded roll-call vote on each recommendation within 60 days.

Step 3

"Comply-or-Explain" Mandate

If Council rejects any proposal with ≥75% assembly consensus, it must issue a formal Written Statement of Findings justifying the decision within 30 days.

Step 4

Automatic Ballot Trigger

If Council fails to act within 60 days or rejects consensus recommendations, the blueprint is automatically referred to the next general election ballot.

Step 5

Binding RFP & Site Plan Criteria

All adopted assembly recommendations become mandatory design parameters for any future Request for Proposals (RFP) for the Junction lot.

Step 6

12-Month Citizen Implementation Panel

A 3–5 member lottery-selected alumni panel oversees staff progress with mandatory biannual public accountability hearings.
